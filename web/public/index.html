            SERVICENOW AVR · CODESECURITY

            False positive review

            Submit one AVIT or a list. Each AVIT is reviewed on its own repository and branch, gets its own Word report, and is closed or annotated on its own.

            AVIT numbers (AVIT plus 7 to 15 digits, e.g. AVIT000000095938462 or AVIT0042599) or catalog tasks (e.g. SCTASK14120565), separated by commas, spaces, or new lines (up to 50 AVITs). For an SCTASK, every AVIT in its description is reviewed, then the task gets each report attached and a “Closed by Threatlint” comment, and is closed once every AVIT is decided.

            False positive · Word report attached, closed (state 3 / substate 3) with “Closed by Threatlint as a false positive, see attached report.”, Semgrep finding ignored, and for SAST/Secrets the rule tuned once.
            True positive · Word report attached, left open with “True positive, see attached report.”
            Ambiguous · Word report attached, left open with “Ambiguous, see attached report.”
